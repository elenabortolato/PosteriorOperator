======================================================================================
Reading
======================================================================================
  The measured picture is the reverse of the expected one, and the reversal
  is the useful result.

  In the FIXED-ARCHITECTURE regime -- the one predicted to give root-n -- the
  spread does NOT shrink at root-n: 'sd * sqrt(n)' grows steadily and the RMSE
  plateaus. Two things cause it. A rank-8 network is not a correctly specified
  parametric family for this operator (its exact spectrum is infinite), so
  there is an approximation floor no amount of data removes; and the residual
  spread across replicates is driven by where the non-convex fit lands, which
  is a function of initialisation rather than of n and so does not shrink at
  all. The M-estimation argument needs a unique, well-separated population
  minimiser that the optimiser actually reaches -- with a neural parametrisation
  neither premise holds, and that gap is what shows up here.

  In the GROWING-CAPACITY regime -- the one predicted to be slower -- the error
  keeps falling and tracks root-n over this range, because the approximation
  error is still the dominant, shrinking term.

  Consequence for the interval: the draw-bootstrap degrades from usable to
  badly overconfident as n grows (90% nominal covering about half the time at
  the largest n), and it degrades precisely BECAUSE it is correct about the
  part it measures. Its width falls like n^{-1/2} while the true spread
  plateaus, so the two diverge. Report it as a Monte Carlo error bar on the
  averaging step, which is what it is, not as a confidence interval for
  T_f(y_0).

  For the write-up this suggests stating the fixed-architecture result as an
  interval for the best rank-d approximation of T_f(y_0) -- conditional on the
  learned subspace -- rather than for T_f(y_0) itself, and being explicit that
  optimisation variability is a third error source alongside approximation and
  sampling. It is checkable, and it is what the experiment supports.
