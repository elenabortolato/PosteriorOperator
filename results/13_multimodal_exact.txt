  The prediction this was built to test does not hold. NPE is ahead at
  every p, by factors of 3.74, 4.47 and 2.65, and the trend is not monotone,
  so neither the operator's supposed advantage nor a mixture-capacity
  penalty shows up.

  The figure says why. NPE recovers both humps cleanly even at sixty-four
  modes; the operator puts a spurious peak in the VALLEY between them. The
  original argument confused support with weights. Prior atoms do sit near
  both modes, but their weights come from a rank-d truncation, which is a
  smooth approximation to the ratio and cannot turn sharply enough. Sharp
  multimodality is expensive for this estimator, not free -- the same
  smoothing that puts fifteen spurious islands in the SIR panel of example
  06, seen here against an exact posterior instead of a quadrature one.

  What this table CANNOT settle: chi^2 here is 21 to 26, which example 09
  already calls NPE-favourable, so multimodality and concentration are
  confounded in the numbers. The refutation rests on the figure, where
  misplaced mass between modes is visible whatever chi^2 is doing.

  The prior-only column remains the control, and both methods beat it
  everywhere, so neither is degenerate.
