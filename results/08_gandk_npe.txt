Cost of one more functional, after training
====================================================================================
  300 observations, NPE using 4000 draws each.

         cold (ms/obs)   warm (ms/obs)    f evaluated at
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   NCP          0.0572          0.0052       20,000 pts
   NPE          0.2486          0.0078    1,200,000 pts
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  cold: 4x in NCP's favour, warm: 2x
  The structural reason is the last column. The operator's atoms are the same
  stored prior draws for every observation, so a new f is evaluated once for
  the whole batch and each observation costs one dot product. NPE's draws are
  per-observation, so f must be evaluated n_obs x n_draws times and the count
  grows with the batch. The gap widens with the number of observations queried,
  not with the number of functionals.

Reading
====================================================================================
  Read the W1 column, not the mean column. Under this prior g is only weakly
  identified by 100 observations, so a method can land the posterior mean and
  still be badly wrong about the shape; W1 against the reference catches that
  and the mean error does not.

  Everything here is scored against an ABC reference, which is itself an
  estimate. The 'ref floor' column says how far that reference moves when its
  own tolerance is tightened fourfold. A method-to-method gap smaller than the
  floor is not evidence of anything.
