Reading
================================================================================================
  Read DOWN a 'W1@d' column for the effect of budget at a fixed rank, and
  ACROSS a row for the effect of rank at a fixed budget. Example 11 predicts
  the low-rank columns are flat in the budget (truncation-limited) and the
  high-rank column improves (estimation-limited); whether that survives on a
  model with no closed-form ratio is the point of running it here.

  Compare the NPE column down the same rows. If NPE keeps improving with
  budget while the operator plateaus at every rank, the constraint is
  neither truncation nor estimation but capacity -- the third knob example 11
  identified at high chi^2 and did not test.

  Nothing below the reference-drift figure printed above should be read as
  a real difference.
