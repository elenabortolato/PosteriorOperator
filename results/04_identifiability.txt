2. chi^2 and the near-deterministic-simulator regime
================================================================================
  As the observation noise shrinks, the data pin the identified direction
  down exactly, the joint law concentrates near a lower-dimensional
  manifold, the density ratio leaves L^2(pi x mu) and sigma_1 -> 1.

  sigma_1 climbing towards 1 while chi^2 keeps growing with the rank is the
  warning sign. The remedy in that regime is to add observation noise or
  coarsen y before fitting, not to raise the rank.

================================================================================
3. How the low-rank posterior degrades as the data get more informative
================================================================================
  The same MA(2) model with a longer series gives a tighter posterior. The
  posterior location stays accurate; the reported spread does not.

 series length T  concentration    chi^2   mean RMSE  sd ratio   sd ratio
                (prior/post sd)                         signed    clipped
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
               5           1.4x     1.05      0.2542      1.18       1.20
              15           2.2x     3.58      0.2726      1.21       1.59
              50           4.7x     8.29      0.1308      1.63       2.92
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A sd ratio of 1.00 would be exact. Two things are visible:
   * the ratio grows with concentration -- a posterior much tighter than the
     prior needs a large chi^2, so the truncated spectrum falls short;
   * clipping negative masses makes it markedly worse, because the negative
     mass is what carves probability away from the prior's tails. Moment
     functionals should therefore use the signed estimator, which is what
     Eq. (2) actually specifies.

  Practical reading: the low-rank operator is at its best for moderately
  informative experiments and for low-order functionals. For tail quantiles
  under highly informative data, either raise the rank substantially or fall
  back to direct regression on the one functional wanted.
