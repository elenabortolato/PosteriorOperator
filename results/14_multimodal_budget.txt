Reading
================================================================================================
  Read the 'valley' column down each budget block. It is the mass the
  estimate puts between the modes, where the truth has a minimum, so it
  measures the actual defect rather than an aggregate. If it shrinks with
  rank, the smoothing explanation is right and the fix is spectral terms.
  If it does not, sharp multimodality is a limitation of a low-rank ratio
  model and no budget removes it.

  Compare the NCP W1 column against the NPE row. Closing the gap is what
  'achievable by spending more' would mean; note also the fit-time column,
  since rank costs O(n d^2) and that is the price of the fix if it works.
